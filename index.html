About

I study the genomic determinants of antimicrobial resistance (AMR) in bacteria, including coding regions, regulatory elements and plasmids. My work combines large-scale genomic analysis, data analytics and machine learning to understand how resistance evolves and spreads.

My PhD thesis is titled Computational Approaches and Data Analytics for Exploring Antimicrobial Resistance Determinants in Bacterial Coding Regions, Regulatory Elements, and Plasmids, supervised by Dr. S. Suma Mohan.
